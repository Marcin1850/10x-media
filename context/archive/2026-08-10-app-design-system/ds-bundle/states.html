Two slots that usually get skipped, and both sit on the money path. Needs your decision is neither an error nor a success — nothing failed and nothing was charged, the app is asking. Unknown must read as neither 0 nor an error: showing zero credits when the balance merely failed to load is a false statement about the user’s account.

* Failure copy must branch on the outcome. A caption-less video is charged one credit, so a blanket “no credit was taken” is sometimes a lie about the user’s money. The error card reports the charge; it does not cause it.
