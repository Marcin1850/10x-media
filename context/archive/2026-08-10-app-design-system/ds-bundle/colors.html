★ attention is outside the shadcn contract. It means one thing only: the app is waiting for your decision about spending credits — the long-video cost gate. Amber appears there and nowhere else. Do not reuse it for badges, highlights or emphasis.
--accent is deliberately identical to --muted, which is what stock shadcn does. It is a hover surface, not a state.

Light is unverified. Every state specimen was drawn on dark. One known failure already: --attention as text on the 12% attention tint is 6.46:1 on dark but 4.25:1 on light — below AA. Dark escapes it with a lightened text variant that light has no counterpart for. Fix before any theme toggle ships.
